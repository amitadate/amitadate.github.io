I am a PhD candidate in Computer Engineering at Northwestern University, advised by [Dr. Aggelos K. Katsaggelos](https://www.mccormick.northwestern.edu/research-faculty/directory/profiles/katsaggelos-aggelos.html). My research focuses on **Medical Imaging and Deep Learning**, with emphasis on computer vision for healthcare applications.

I work at the [Image and Video Processing Lab (IVPL)](https://sites.northwestern.edu/ivpl/), developing models for medical image analysis and activity recognition. Prior to that, I completed my MS in Artificial Intelligence from Northwestern (2019), advised by [Dr. Kristian Hammond](https://www.mccormick.northwestern.edu/research-faculty/directory/profiles/hammond-kristian.html) and B.Tech. in Computer Science from Vellore Institute of Technology (2018), advised by [Dr. B. K. Tripathy](http://www.bktripathy.co.in/).
